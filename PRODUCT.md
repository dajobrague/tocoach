# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

**Primary: fitness trainers** (tenant owners) managing their own roster of clients. ~42 tenants, ~289 clients in production. Spanish-speaking; the entire trainer UI is in Spanish.

**Secondary: clients** (end users) on `/[slug]/*`, and **platform admins** on `/admin/*`. Both are separate audiences with their own URL trees, sessions and design surfaces — out of scope for trainer-app work unless stated.

Trainers arrive at a client profile to do one of three jobs:

1. **Program or edit the week** — the highest-frequency job. Land, go to Entrenamientos, work.
2. **Check in on a client** — scan whether they trained, logged, and are on track.
3. **Set up a new client** — once per client: forms, access, initial program.

Looking up a single fact and leaving is _not_ a common job. The profile is a workspace, not a lookup.

## Product Purpose

TopCoach is a multi-tenant SaaS that lets an independent fitness trainer run their whole practice — programming, nutrition, habit forms, progress charts, video review and chat — under their own brand, without building software. Success is a trainer spending their time coaching rather than assembling spreadsheets, and a client who opens one branded app instead of five tools.

## Positioning

The trainer's own brand is the product surface, not TopCoach's. Every tenant supplies a theme (`tenants.theme_json`) that drives the client portal, and — as of the F0 work on this branch — the trainer app itself. A neighboring product that ships one fixed look cannot truthfully copy this: the design system's job is to stay correct and legible across ~42 arbitrary trainer-chosen palettes, including ones with poor contrast.

## Operating Context

- **Primary surface: desktop inside a GoHighLevel iframe.** GHL's own chrome already consumes vertical space, so every pixel the app spends on persistent header furniture is taken from the work area. This is the constraint that dominates layout decisions.
- **Also used:** desktop with the app in its own tab, and tablet widths (in-gym).
- **Not a trainer surface:** phone. Client-side mobile PWA use exists, but trainers do not program from a phone. Tablet is the narrow-width case to design for, not 375px.
- Client portal may itself be embedded in iframes, which is why client sessions use `SameSite=None` and a `Bearer` fallback.
- The client profile is 7 lazily-loaded tabs (Entrenamientos, Gráficas, NEAT, Nutrición, Suplementos, Formularios, Acceso). The tabs are the work; the header is the frame around it.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Stack:** Next.js 15 App Router, HeroUI v2, Tailwind v4, TypeScript with `strict` + `noUncheckedIndexedAccess` + `exactOptionalPropertyTypes`. Icons via `@iconify/react` (Solar set; `fluent` for a few). Supabase for all data.
- **Theming:** tenant colors reach the app as HSL triples in `--heroui-*` variables generated by `lib/theme/render-css.ts`. Foreground colors are chosen by measured contrast (`lib/theme/color-utils.ts`), not hardcoded to white. Semantic tokens (`bg-content1`, `border-default-200`, `text-foreground`, `text-default-500`, `color="primary"`) are the only legitimate way to express color in trainer UI. A regression test at `features/trainer/__tests__/no-hardcoded-brand-classes.test.ts` pins this, but **only for the five shell files F0 migrated** (nutrition-update-banner, side-shell, top-shell, plantillas-dropdown, pending-reviews-card) and only for the specific literals retired there. It is not a global guard: `components/dashboard/clients-content.tsx` and the whole client-profile tree still carry `bg-white`, `gray-`, `blue-`, `green-50`, `purple-50`, `orange-50` literals. Migrating a surface means extending this test to cover it.
- **Legacy theme data:** production tenants carry partial `theme_json` shapes. Theme reads must deep-default and guard, never assume a full object.
- **Client profile data:** `GET /api/clients/[clientId]/profile` returns identity only — `name`, `firstName`, `lastName`, `nickName`, `email`, `avatar`, `phone`, `status`, `joinedDate`, `age`, `dob`, `sex`, `heightCm`, `occupation`, `goals[]`, `nationalId`, `location{city,state,country,zip}`. **No program, weight, adherence or session data.** Any header showing those requires a new query and is a product decision, not a styling one.
- `sex` and `heightCm` are present in the payload but not currently surfaced anywhere in the header.
- Profile navigation state lives in the URL (`?tab`, `?sub`, `?m`, `?modal`) via `useUrlEnum` / `useModalParam`.
- **Undecided:** whether the profile header should ever show training/adherence state. Not currently possible without new data plumbing.

## Brand Commitments

- The product name is TopCoach, but the trainer app must read as the **trainer's** brand: tenant primary color, tenant logo, tenant typography variables. TopCoach branding is deliberately absent from tenant-facing surfaces.
- UI language is Spanish (es-ES), including dates (`toLocaleDateString("es-ES")`).
- Existing kit primitives that new work must reuse rather than reinvent: `OutlineChip`, `CenteredState`, `IconTile`, `SegmentedControl` in `components/shared/`.

## Product Principles

1. **The tabs are the product; the frame is not.** Persistent chrome must justify every pixel it takes from the work area, because GHL already took the first slice.
2. **Identity is permanent; the rest is on demand.** Asked in the abstract, trainers want every fact visible at once; shown the result, they chose a collapsed ficha (Sep 9). Confirming _which client you are on_ — avatar, name, status, goals — is always on screen. Physical facts and contact fold behind a "Ficha" toggle that remembers its state per trainer. Treat the seen-it verdict as the real requirement, not the stated one.
3. **Correct under any tenant palette.** A design is only done when it survives a dark brand, a pale brand, and a legacy partial theme without a hardcoded fallback.
4. **Semantic tokens or nothing.** Color literals are a regression, not a shortcut.
5. **Design for tablet-up, not phone.** Narrow means ~768px, not 375px; effort spent on phone layouts for trainer screens is effort spent on a surface nobody uses.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Contrast is a load-bearing product requirement, not a checkbox: arbitrary tenant colors mean foregrounds must be computed against measured contrast (already implemented in F0) rather than assumed.
- HeroUI focus rings must survive. Existing code strips them with `focus:outline-none` in places; that is a defect to fix, not a style to match.
- Tab bars must expose real `role="tablist"`/`role="tab"`/`aria-selected` semantics and be keyboard-navigable.
